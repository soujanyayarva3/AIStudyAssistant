About AI Study Assistant

Learn smarter, organize better, and achieve your academic goals with the power of Artificial Intelligence.

📚 About the Project

AI Study Assistant is an intelligent learning management platform designed to help students organize their studies and learn more effectively. It combines traditional study tools with Artificial Intelligence to provide personalized academic assistance.

Students can manage subjects, create study plans, write notes, clarify doubts using AI, generate summaries and quizzes, and monitor their learning progress from a single platform.

✨ Key Features

🤖

AI Doubt Assistant

Get intelligent explanations and answers to your academic doubts.

📝

Smart Notes

Create and organize your study notes in one convenient place.

📖

AI Summaries

Generate concise summaries from study materials and documents.

🧠

AI Quizzes

Practice your knowledge with automatically generated quizzes.

📅

Study Plans

Organize your subjects and tasks with structured study plans.

📊

Progress Tracking

Monitor your learning activities and understand your study progress.
